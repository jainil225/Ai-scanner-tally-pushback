How it works

1. Add bills (photos, PDFs or a ZIP).
2. Scan — AI reads each bill and builds a Tally voucher using your company's ledgers.
3. Check the voucher next to the bill.
4. Push — it lands in Tally as an Optional voucher.

In TallyPrime, open Day Book (or Display More Reports → Exception Reports → Optional Vouchers), open the entry, check it and press Ctrl+L to make it Regular. Until then it does not affect your books.
